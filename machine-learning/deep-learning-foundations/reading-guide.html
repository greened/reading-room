Machine learning · Deep-learning foundations

In-depth landmark list for this subtopic. PDFopen copy available · DOIpublisher link only.

  1. Learning representations by back-propagating errors
    DOIDOI
    Rumelhart, Hinton, Williams, 1986, Nature. Established backpropagation as the practical way to train multi-layer nets.
  2. Gradient-Based Learning Applied to Document Recognition
    DOIDOI
    LeCun, Bottou, Bengio, Haffner, 1998, Proc. IEEE. LeNet-5 and end-to-end gradient-trained CNNs.
  3. Reducing the Dimensionality of Data with Neural Networks
    PDFDOI · PDF
    Hinton, Salakhutdinov, 2006, Science. Greedy layer-wise pretraining reignited deep learning.
  4. Understanding the difficulty of training deep feedforward neural networks
    PDFpage · PDF
    Glorot, Bengio, 2010, AISTATS. Xavier/Glorot initialization.
  5. Dropout: A Simple Way to Prevent Neural Networks from Overfitting
    PDFpage · PDF
    Srivastava, Hinton, Krizhevsky, Sutskever, Salakhutdinov, 2014, JMLR. Dropout regularization.
  6. Batch Normalization
    PDFarXiv · PDF
    Ioffe, Szegedy, 2015, ICML. Normalizing activations to train deep nets faster and more stably.
  7. Adam: A Method for Stochastic Optimization
    PDFarXiv · PDF
    Kingma, Ba, 2015, ICLR. The default adaptive-gradient optimizer.
  8. Deep Residual Learning for Image Recognition
    PDFarXiv · PDF
    He, Zhang, Ren, Sun, 2016, CVPR. Residual/skip connections; scales to 100+ layers (and into transformers).
