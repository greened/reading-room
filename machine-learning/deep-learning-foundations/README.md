# Machine learning · Deep-learning foundations

- **Learning representations by back-propagating errors** — Rumelhart, Hinton, Williams, 1986, Nature. Established backpropagation as the practical way to train multi-layer nets. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/323533a0)
- **Gradient-Based Learning Applied to Document Recognition** — LeCun, Bottou, Bengio, Haffner, 1998, Proc. IEEE. LeNet-5 and end-to-end gradient-trained CNNs. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/5.726791)
- **Reducing the Dimensionality of Data with Neural Networks** — Hinton, Salakhutdinov, 2006, Science. Greedy layer-wise pretraining reignited deep learning. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1127647) · [PDF](https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~hinton/absps/science.pdf)
- **Understanding the difficulty of training deep feedforward neural networks** — Glorot, Bengio, 2010, AISTATS. Xavier/Glorot initialization. [page](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v9/glorot10a.html) · [PDF](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v9/glorot10a/glorot10a.pdf)
- **Dropout: A Simple Way to Prevent Neural Networks from Overfitting** — Srivastava, Hinton, Krizhevsky, Sutskever, Salakhutdinov, 2014, JMLR. Dropout regularization. [page](https://jmlr.org/papers/v15/srivastava14a.html) · [PDF](https://jmlr.org/papers/volume15/srivastava14a/srivastava14a.pdf)
- **Batch Normalization** — Ioffe, Szegedy, 2015, ICML. Normalizing activations to train deep nets faster and more stably. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.03167) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1502.03167)
- **Adam: A Method for Stochastic Optimization** — Kingma, Ba, 2015, ICLR. The default adaptive-gradient optimizer. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.6980) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1412.6980)
- **Deep Residual Learning for Image Recognition** — He, Zhang, Ren, Sun, 2016, CVPR. Residual/skip connections; scales to 100+ layers (and into transformers). [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.03385) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1512.03385)
- **Delving Deep into Rectifiers (PReLU / Kaiming init)** — He, Zhang, Ren, Sun, 2015, ICCV. PReLU + He initialization for stably training very deep rectifier nets. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.01852) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1502.01852)
- **Layer Normalization** — Ba, Kiros, Hinton, 2016, arXiv. Batch-size-independent normalization, foundational to RNNs and Transformers. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1607.06450) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1607.06450)
- **On the Importance of Initialization and Momentum in Deep Learning** — Sutskever, Martens, Dahl, Hinton, 2013, ICML. Careful init + Nesterov momentum lets plain SGD train deep nets. [page](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v28/sutskever13.html) · [PDF](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v28/sutskever13.pdf)
- **Approximation by Superpositions of a Sigmoidal Function** — Cybenko, 1989, Math. of Control, Signals and Systems. First proof that one-hidden-layer sigmoidal nets are universal approximators. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02551274)
- **Gaussian Error Linear Units (GELUs)** — Hendrycks, Gimpel, 2016, arXiv. The GELU activation, now the default nonlinearity in Transformers. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.08415) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1606.08415)
