# Machine learning · Training, scaling & practice

- **Distilling the Knowledge in a Neural Network** — Hinton, Vinyals, Dean, 2015, NeurIPS Workshop. Knowledge distillation — compressing large models into deployable ones. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.02531) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1503.02531)
- **Scaling Laws for Neural Language Models** — Kaplan et al., 2020, arXiv. Loss follows smooth power laws in model size, data, and compute. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.08361) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2001.08361)
- **Training Compute-Optimal Large Language Models (Chinchilla)** — Hoffmann et al., 2022, NeurIPS. Corrected the compute-optimal recipe (scale data and parameters together). [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.15556) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2203.15556)
- **Mixed Precision Training** — Micikevicius et al., 2018, ICLR. FP16 training with loss scaling + FP32 master weights; half the memory. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.03740) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1710.03740)
- **Decoupled Weight Decay Regularization (AdamW)** — Loshchilov, Hutter, 2019, ICLR. Decoupled weight decay; the default Transformer optimizer. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.05101) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1711.05101)
- **SGDR: Warm Restarts** — Loshchilov, Hutter, 2017, ICLR. Cosine-annealing LR schedules with warm restarts. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.03983) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1608.03983)
- **Deep RL from Human Preferences** — Christiano et al., 2017, NeurIPS. Foundational RLHF: training from human preference comparisons. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03741) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1706.03741)
- **Training Language Models to Follow Instructions (InstructGPT)** — Ouyang et al., 2022, NeurIPS. RLHF instruction tuning; the recipe behind ChatGPT-style assistants. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.02155) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2203.02155)
- **LoRA: Low-Rank Adaptation of Large Language Models** — Hu et al., 2022, ICLR. Low-rank adapters that slashed fine-tuning cost; launched PEFT. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.09685) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2106.09685)
- **Emergent Abilities of Large Language Models** — Wei et al., 2022, TMLR. Scale-driven capabilities appearing abruptly beyond a threshold. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.07682) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2206.07682)
