Machine Learning — Landmark Papers

A cross-cutting survey of the field's most important papers. Folder: reading-room/machine-learning/ · canonical references live in the sibling subtopic dirs (transformers in ../transformers/).

Roughly a dozen papers that define modern machine learning, in a sensible reading order (foundations and recurrent nets, the ImageNet breakthrough, embeddings and generation, deep RL, residual learning, diffusion, scaling). Each entry points to the subtopic dir that treats it in depth.   Legend: PDFopen copy available (see fetch.sh) · DOIpublisher link only.

  1. Learning representations by back-propagating errors — Rumelhart, Hinton, Williams
    DOINature 1986 · doi:10.1038/323533a0 · → deep-learning-foundations/
    Established backpropagation as the practical way to train multi-layer networks — the algorithmic bedrock.
  2. Long Short-Term Memory — Hochreiter, Schmidhuber
    DOINeural Computation 1997 · doi:10.1162/neco.1997.9.8.1735 · → sequence-models/
    The gated recurrent cell that solved vanishing gradients and dominated sequence modeling for two decades.
  3. ImageNet Classification with Deep Convolutional Neural Networks (AlexNet) — Krizhevsky, Sutskever, Hinton
    PDFNeurIPS 2012 · PDF · → computer-vision/
    The ImageNet win that started the modern deep-learning boom.
  4. Efficient Estimation of Word Representations in Vector Space (word2vec) — Mikolov, Chen, Corrado, Dean
    PDFICLR Workshop 2013 · arXiv:1301.3781 · → sequence-models/
    Made dense, semantically meaningful word embeddings cheap and popular for NLP.
  5. Auto-Encoding Variational Bayes (VAE) — Kingma, Welling
    PDFICLR 2014 · arXiv:1312.6114 · → generative-models/
    The VAE and reparameterization trick, founding modern deep latent-variable generative modeling.
  6. Generative Adversarial Nets — Goodfellow et al.
    PDFNeurIPS 2014 · arXiv:1406.2661 · → generative-models/
    Framed generation as an adversarial game and drove a decade of high-fidelity image synthesis.
  7. Sequence to Sequence Learning with Neural Networks — Sutskever, Vinyals, Le
    PDFNeurIPS 2014 · arXiv:1409.3215 · → sequence-models/
    The encoder-decoder framework for mapping sequences to sequences — the direct precursor to attention.
  8. Human-level control through deep reinforcement learning (DQN) — Mnih et al.
    PDFNature 2015 · doi:10.1038/nature14236 · → reinforcement-learning/
    Learned Atari from pixels, founding deep reinforcement learning.
  9. Deep Residual Learning for Image Recognition (ResNet) — He, Zhang, Ren, Sun
    PDFCVPR 2016 · arXiv:1512.03385 · → deep-learning-foundations/
    Residual/skip connections let networks scale to 100+ layers — a primitive that carries into transformers.
  10. Mastering the game of Go with deep neural networks and tree search (AlphaGo) — Silver et al.
    DOINature 2016 · doi:10.1038/nature16961 · → reinforcement-learning/
    Combined deep policy/value networks with MCTS to beat top humans at Go — a landmark for learned search.
  11. Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models (DDPM) — Ho, Jain, Abbeel
    PDFNeurIPS 2020 · arXiv:2006.11239 · → generative-models/
    The DDPM formulation launched diffusion models as the dominant image-generation approach.
  12. Scaling Laws for Neural Language Models — Kaplan et al.
    PDF2020 · arXiv:2001.08361 · → training-and-scaling/
    Showed loss follows smooth power laws in model size, data, and compute — the empirical basis for the scale-up era.
