Machine learning · Reinforcement learning

In-depth landmark list for this subtopic. PDFopen copy available · DOIpublisher link only.

  1. Human-level control through deep reinforcement learning (DQN)
    PDFDOI · PDF
    Mnih et al., 2015, Nature. Learned Atari from pixels; founded deep RL.
  2. Mastering the game of Go with deep neural networks and tree search (AlphaGo)
    DOIDOI
    Silver et al., 2016, Nature. Deep policy/value nets + MCTS beat top humans at Go.
  3. Proximal Policy Optimization Algorithms (PPO)
    PDFarXiv · PDF
    Schulman, Wolski, Dhariwal, Radford, Klimov, 2017, arXiv. The robust policy-gradient method that became the RL/RLHF default.
  4. Learning to Predict by the Methods of Temporal Differences
    DOIDOI
    Sutton, 1988, Machine Learning. Introduced TD learning, RL's foundational bootstrapping method.
  5. Q-learning
    DOIDOI
    Watkins, Dayan, 1992, Machine Learning. The canonical model-free off-policy control algorithm, with convergence proof.
  6. Simple Statistical Gradient-Following Algorithms (REINFORCE)
    DOIDOI
    Williams, 1992, Machine Learning. The REINFORCE policy-gradient family.
  7. Asynchronous Methods for Deep RL (A3C)
    PDFarXiv · PDF
    Mnih et al., 2016, ICML. Lightweight asynchronous actor-critic beating prior deep-RL SOTA.
  8. Continuous Control with Deep RL (DDPG)
    PDFarXiv · PDF
    Lillicrap et al., 2016, ICLR. Deep Q-learning extended to continuous action spaces.
  9. Trust Region Policy Optimization (TRPO)
    PDFarXiv · PDF
    Schulman et al., 2015, ICML. Monotonic-improvement guarantees for stable policy optimization.
  10. AlphaZero: Mastering Chess and Shogi by Self-Play
    PDFarXiv · PDF
    Silver et al., 2017, arXiv. Superhuman chess/shogi/Go from self-play with one general algorithm.
  11. MuZero: Mastering Atari, Go, Chess and Shogi by Planning with a Learned Model
    PDFarXiv · PDF
    Schrittwieser et al., 2020, Nature. Matched AlphaZero without being given the rules.
