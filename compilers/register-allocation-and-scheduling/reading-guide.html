Compilers · Register allocation & instruction scheduling

  1. Register Allocation and Spilling via Graph Coloring
    PDFDOI · PDF
    Gregory J. Chaitin, 1982, SIGPLAN Compiler Construction. The original graph-coloring formulation.
  2. Improvements to Graph Coloring Register Allocation
    DOIDOI
    Briggs, Cooper, Torczon, 1994, ACM TOPLAS. Optimistic coloring and better spill heuristics.
  3. Iterated Register Coalescing
    PDFDOI · PDF
    Lal George, Andrew W. Appel, 1996, ACM TOPLAS. Reconciles move-coalescing with colorability.
  4. Linear Scan Register Allocation
    PDFDOI · PDF
    Poletto, Sarkar, 1999, ACM TOPLAS. The fast non-coloring allocator that became the JIT workhorse.
  5. Trace Scheduling: A Technique for Global Microcode Compaction
    PDFDOI · PDF
    Joseph A. Fisher, 1981, IEEE TC. Origin of global instruction scheduling and the VLIW philosophy.
  6. Software Pipelining: An Effective Scheduling Technique for VLIW Machines
    PDFDOI · PDF
    Monica S. Lam, 1988, PLDI. Modulo scheduling for loops.
