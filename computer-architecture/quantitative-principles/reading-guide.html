Computer architecture · Quantitative principles

The laws and models that frame architecture as a quantitative discipline — Amdahl, Dennard, the memory wall, roofline, and the end-of-scaling arguments.

In-depth landmark list for this subtopic. PDFopen copy available · DOIpublisher link only.

  1. Validity of the Single Processor Approach to Achieving Large Scale Computing Capabilities
    DOIDOI
    Gene M. Amdahl, 1967, AFIPS. Origin of Amdahl's Law: the speedup bound from a serial fraction.
  2. The Case for the Reduced Instruction Set Computer
    DOIDOI
    Patterson, Ditzel, 1980, SIGARCH CAN. The manifesto that framed the RISC-vs-CISC debate.
  3. Design of Ion-Implanted MOSFETs with Very Small Physical Dimensions
    DOIDOI
    Dennard, Gaensslen, Yu, Rideout, Bassous, LeBlanc, 1974, IEEE JSSC. States Dennard scaling — the device-physics rule whose end reshaped everything.
  4. Hitting the Memory Wall: Implications of the Obvious
    DOIDOI
    Wulf, McKee, 1995, SIGARCH CAN. Coined the "memory wall."
  5. Amdahl's Law in the Multicore Era
    PDFDOI · PDF
    Hill, Marty, 2008, IEEE Computer. Amdahl for chip multiprocessors (symmetric/asymmetric/dynamic).
  6. The Future of Microprocessors
    DOIDOI
    Borkar, Chien, 2011, CACM. Energy efficiency, not transistor count, is the fundamental limiter.
  7. Roofline: An Insightful Visual Performance Model for Multicore Architectures
    PDFDOI · PDF
    Williams, Waterman, Patterson, 2009, CACM. Operational intensity vs compute/bandwidth ceilings.
  8. Dark Silicon and the End of Multicore Scaling
    PDFDOI · PDF
    Esmaeilzadeh, Blem, St. Amant, Sankaralingam, Burger, 2011, ISCA. Post-Dennard power limits cap usable cores.
  9. A Mechanistic Performance Model for Superscalar Out-of-Order Processors
    PDFDOI · PDF
    Eyerman, Eeckhout, Karkhanis, Smith, 2009, ACM TOCS. Interval analysis explaining superscalar performance mechanistically.
  10. A New Golden Age for Computer Architecture
    PDFDOI · PDF
    Hennessy, Patterson, 2019, CACM. Turing Lecture: the turn to domain-specific architectures.
  11. Power Struggles: Revisiting the RISC vs. CISC Debate on Contemporary ARM and x86 Architectures
    PDFDOI · PDF
    Blem, Menon, Sankaralingam, 2013, HPCA. Empirically shows ISA is largely irrelevant to power/performance on modern implementations.
