Programming languages · Semantics & language design

In-depth landmark list for this subtopic. PDFopen copy available · DOIpublisher link only.

  1. The Next 700 Programming Languages
    PDFDOI · PDF
    P. J. Landin, 1966, CACM. Frames the whole family of languages around a small applicative core (ISWIM).
  2. An Axiomatic Basis for Computer Programming
    DOIDOI
    C. A. R. Hoare, 1969, CACM. Introduces Hoare logic and the pre/postcondition triple.
  3. A Structural Approach to Operational Semantics
    PDFDOI · PDF
    Gordon D. Plotkin, 1981/2004, JLAP. Founds structural operational semantics (SOS).
  4. Abstract Interpretation
    PDFDOI · PDF
    Patrick & Radhia Cousot, 1977, POPL. The lattice/Galois-connection theory underlying sound static analysis.
  5. Toward a Mathematical Semantics for Computer Languages
    PDFPDF
    Scott, Strachey, 1971, Oxford PRG-6. The founding statement of denotational semantics.
  6. Definitional Interpreters for Higher-Order Programming Languages
    DOIDOI
    John C. Reynolds, 1972, ACM Annual Conf. Defunctionalization; meta-circular vs definitional interpreters.
  7. A Syntactic Approach to Type Soundness
    DOIDOI
    Wright, Felleisen, 1994, Information and Computation. The standard progress-and-preservation soundness method.
  8. The Syntactic Theories of Sequential Control and State
    DOIDOI
    Felleisen, Hieb, 1992, TCS. Reduction semantics of control and state via evaluation contexts. 90014-7)
  9. Recursive Functions of Symbolic Expressions… (LISP), Part I
    DOIDOI
    John McCarthy, 1960, CACM. Defined LISP; launched functional programming.
  10. Fundamental Concepts in Programming Languages
    DOIDOI
    Christopher Strachey, 1967/2000, HOSC. l-values/r-values, parametric vs ad-hoc polymorphism, first-class values.
